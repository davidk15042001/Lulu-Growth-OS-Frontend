Data Deletion

Effective: 23 September 2026 - Contact: privacy@lulu-ai.tech

Legal entity: Hong Kong Lulu Development Limited

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request to privacy@lulu-ai.tech from the email address associated with your Lulu account. Include the workspace name, account email, provider name, and a description of the data you want deleted.

Workspace and provider data

Connected-provider data can be removed by disconnecting the provider integration inside Lulu and revoking access in the provider account.

What may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for security, billing, tax, legal, dispute-resolution, fraud prevention, audit, backup, or provider-verification purposes. Retained records are handled according to the Privacy Policy.

Response timing

We will acknowledge requests after receipt and process eligible requests within the timeframe required by applicable law.
