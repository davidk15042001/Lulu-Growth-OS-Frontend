Data Deletion

Effective: 23 September 2026 - Contact: privacy@lulu-ai.tech

Legal entity: Hong Kong Lulu Development Limited

This page explains how individuals, workspace administrators, and connected-provider users can request deletion of data handled by Lulu AI.

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request to privacy@lulu-ai.tech from the email address associated with your Lulu account or connected provider account. Include the workspace name, account email, provider name, and a short description of the data you want deleted.

Workspace and provider data

Lulu may process data for customer workspaces. If your data belongs to a customer workspace, we may route your request to the workspace owner or ask for additional verification before acting. Connected-provider data can also be removed by disconnecting the provider integration inside Lulu and revoking Lulu's access in the provider account.

What may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for security, billing, tax, legal, dispute-resolution, fraud prevention, audit, backup, or provider-verification purposes. Retained records are limited to what is necessary and are handled according to the Privacy Policy.

Response timing

We will acknowledge requests after receipt and process eligible deletion requests within the timeframe required by applicable law. We may ask for information needed to verify identity, locate the relevant workspace, or confirm authority to request deletion.
