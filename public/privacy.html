Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 September 2026 · Contact: privacy@lulu-ai.cn

1. Scope and controller

This policy explains how the operator of Lulu AI processes personal data when people use lulu-ai.cn, create an account, connect business systems or communicate through the service. The contracting operator and address shown in an executed Lulu order form or company notice are the responsible controller details for that customer relationship.

2. Data we process

We process account and company-profile data, authentication and security events, billing and transaction references, connected-provider account data, customer-supplied knowledge, communications, operational records, generated content and technical usage data. Payment card credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by Lulu.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to provide and secure the service, perform requested automated actions, administer accounts and billing, communicate about the service, prevent abuse and meet legal duties. Depending on the context, processing is based on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent or a legal obligation.

4. AI and autonomous processing

Lulu uses AI providers to analyse supplied business information and produce or execute permitted outputs. Customer content may be sent to configured processors only as needed for the requested service. Lulu applies workspace isolation, authorization rules, budget controls and execution logs. Customers must not upload data they are not entitled to process.

5. Processors and international transfers

Infrastructure, AI, storage, communications and payment providers may process limited data on Lulu’s behalf. Where data crosses jurisdictions, Lulu uses the transfer mechanism required by applicable law. A current processor list and data-processing agreement can be requested from the privacy contact.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained while an account is active and afterwards only for security, dispute, backup and statutory requirements. Retention varies by record type. Customers may request export, correction or deletion; some billing and audit records must be retained by law. Backup copies expire on their normal protected rotation.

7. Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent supervisory authority. Requests can be sent to the privacy contact and may require identity verification.

8. Security and changes

Lulu uses encryption in transit, access controls, audit logging and tenant-scoped authorization. No system is risk-free. Security reports should follow security.txt. Material policy changes will be announced through the service or by email where required.
